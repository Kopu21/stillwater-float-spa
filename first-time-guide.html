Clarity & Confidence

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I feel claustrophobic inside the pod? ▾
Almost everyone is pleasantly surprised! Pod Alpha is over 8.5 feet long and 5 feet wide. You are in complete control: the lid never locks, opens with a gentle touch, and you can leave internal lights on or door cracked. We also offer Basin Beta, an open-concept room with 9-foot ceilings and no lid whatsoever.
How clean is the float water? ▾
Float water is extraordinarily pure. Over 1,200 pounds of medical-grade Epsom salt creates a saline environment so dense that bacteria and pathogens cannot survive. Furthermore, between every single guest, 100% of the water runs through a 4-stage hospital filtration cycle with 1-micron sediment filters, high-output germicidal UV-C light, and ozone purification.
What happens if I fall asleep inside the pod? ▾
Sleeping is safe and encouraged! With 1,200 lbs of dissolved salt, the water has a specific gravity of 1.28. It is physically impossible to sink. Even if you turn, the dense water will gently rotate you back onto your back.
What should I wear during the float? ▾
Because each chamber is inside your own private, locked suite with an en-suite rain shower, most guests float without clothing to eliminate tactile sensations. However, clean swimsuits are completely welcome if you prefer.
