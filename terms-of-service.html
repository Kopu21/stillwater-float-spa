Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 • Policies Ensuring Sanctuary Safety, Hygiene, and Quiet Integration

1. Sanctuary Safety & Hygiene Standards

Every guest at Stillwater enters an immaculate, calibrated environment supersaturated with 1,200 pounds of pharmaceutical Epsom salt. To preserve mineral purity and ensure absolute hygiene for all floaters:

  • Mandatory Pre-Float Shower: Guests must thoroughly shower with our provided organic body wash and clarifying shampoo before entering float chambers.
  • Hair & Skin Sensitivities: No hair dye, coloring, or tanning treatments may be worn if applied within seven (7) days prior to your session, as pigments leach into salt solutions.
  • Open Wounds & Contraindications: Flotation is prohibited with fresh tattoos, unhealed abrasions, untreated epilepsy, or severe kidney ailments.
  • Zero Intoxication: Use of alcohol, narcotics, or impairment substances before entering a chamber is strictly forbidden.

2. Appointments, Cancellations & Rescheduling

Our chambers require dedicated preparation, individual 4-cycle sanitization, and thermal recalibration for each reservation:

  • Cancellation Notice: Cancellations or rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours prior to appointment time to preserve session credit.
  • Arrival Buffer: First-time guests are requested to arrive 15 minutes prior to appointment time for our guided orientation walkthrough.
  • Late Arrivals: Late arrivals may result in an abbreviated float session to protect following guests' scheduled buffer times.

3. Sanctuary Etiquette & Acoustic Serenity

Stillwater is an intentional acoustic sanctuary. We ask all guests to respect the quietude of our corridors, transition suites, and post-float integration lounge:

  • Mobile devices must remain silenced and stowed within private lockers upon passing the threshold.
  • Footwear is removed at the sanctuary reception threshold to preserve pristine floor cleanliness.
  • Whisper voices are observed in common corridors to protect deep meditative states in adjacent suites.

4. Membership & Package Credits

Float credits, monthly memberships, and multi-session restorative packages are associated with your verified client dashboard. Credits are non-refundable but may be shared with immediate family members or paused upon request with 14 days written notice.
